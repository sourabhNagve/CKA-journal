set-cluster: creates or updates cluster entry in your kubeconfig file, usually ~/.kube/config
 KUBECONFIG=/opt/course/kubeconfigs/merged.yaml k config set-cluster cluster-broken --server=https://192.168.1.50:6443
 ?() – filter operator.

@ – “current item” in the array.

@.name – the name field of the current cluster object.

== "internal-cluster" – match only where name equals "internal-cluster".
